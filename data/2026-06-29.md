In this paper, we provide analytical formulae/procedures for the solutions to Ly$α$ RT equations under three simple geometrical symmetries and investigate the effects of atomic recoil and gas bulk motion. We first study Ly$α$ spectra by solving Ly$α$ RT equations for a static, uniform gas cloud under cylindrical geometry.
